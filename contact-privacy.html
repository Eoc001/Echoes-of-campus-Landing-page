1. Why this notice is provided

This notice explains how Echoes of Campus (“EOC”) handles personal information submitted through the Contact form on https://echoesofcampus.vercel.app.

It supplements the main Privacy Policy.

Please read it before submitting a message.

2. Who is responsible for your information?

Data Controller: Sulaiman Mariam Opeyemi
Initiative: Echoes of Campus
Privacy contact: echoesofcampus01@gmail.com
Website: https://echoesofcampus.vercel.app

3. What information may we collect?

Depending on the options and fields you complete, the Contact form may collect:

  • Your name, if requested and provided.
  • Your email address, if requested and provided.
  • Your telephone number, if requested and provided.
  • The reason for contacting EOC.
  • Your message, story, suggestion, concern or enquiry.
  • Any other information you voluntarily include in your submission.

The form's contact categories include:

  • Suggest a topic.
  • Share a story.
  • Raise a concern.
  • Request an interview.
  • Ask a question.
  • General Enquiry.

Please do not include passwords, financial account details, government identification numbers or unnecessary sensitive personal information in your message.

4. Why do we use this information?

We use Contact form submissions to:

  • Read and respond to enquiries.
  • Review topic suggestions and story ideas.
  • Consider interview requests.
  • Understand and address concerns.
  • Follow up where you have provided a suitable contact method.
  • Manage and improve EOC's community engagement.
  • Maintain appropriate records where necessary for accountability, security or legal purposes.

We do not guarantee that every submission will be published, featured or receive a response.

5. Anonymous submissions

Where the form provides an anonymous option, please understand the difference between the following:

A. Anonymous to the public

You may provide your name or contact information so that authorised EOC administrators can review your submission or contact you.

Your identifying information is not automatically intended for public display. If EOC considers publishing your story or other identifiable contribution, appropriate permission or another lawful basis will be required.

B. Completely anonymous

Where the completely anonymous option is provided and correctly configured, the form should not request or transmit your name, email address or telephone number as part of the submission.

Because we will not have your contact details, we may be unable to identify you, verify additional details or send you a personal response.

Important: Completely anonymous does not mean that no technical information can ever exist. Form providers, hosting providers and other infrastructure services may process technical information such as IP addresses, timestamps, browser information or security logs in accordance with their own systems and policies.

Do not include your identity or other identifying details in the message itself if you want to remain anonymous.

6. Who can access your submission?

Access to raw Contact form submissions should be restricted to the authorised EOC administrative team responsible for reviewing and managing submissions.

Department leads and general members should not have unrestricted access to raw Contact form records.

A submission may be shared with another authorised person only where reasonably necessary to review or address the matter, and subject to appropriate confidentiality and privacy safeguards.

7. Which service receives your submission?

The Contact form currently uses Formspree to receive submissions.

The form is configured with the Formspree endpoint associated with identifier xwlolggo.

Formspree is an external service provider. Its processing, storage, technical logs, retention arrangements and possible international processing are subject to its own service terms and privacy documentation.

The Contact form is not currently connected to Google Sheets or Google Apps Script.

If EOC changes the form provider or introduces a Google integration in the future, the relevant privacy information will be updated before the new arrangement is used.

8. Lawful basis

Depending on the nature of your message, EOC may process the information on the basis of your consent, legitimate interests, steps taken at your request, or another lawful basis recognised by the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

9. Retention

We will not keep Contact form submissions indefinitely.

Submissions should be reviewed and deleted or anonymised when they are no longer needed for their original purpose, follow-up, justified organisational record-keeping or legal claims.

Where no other legal or justified retention period applies, EOC will apply the storage limitation requirements of the NDPA and GAID.

Formspree may also retain submission data or technical records according to its own retention arrangements. We encourage users to review the provider's privacy information.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

  • Access personal data concerning you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of information where legally permitted.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where consent is the lawful basis.
  • Exercise other applicable rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

To make a request, email echoesofcampus01@gmail.com.

If you submitted a completely anonymous message without contact details, we may not be able to identify your submission or provide a personal response.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission at https://ndpc.gov.ng/.
