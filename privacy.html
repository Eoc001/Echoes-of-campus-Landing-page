1. Introduction

Echoes of Campus (“EOC”, “we”, “our” or “us”) respects the privacy of individuals who visit our website, apply to volunteer, contact us, share stories, participate in interviews, or otherwise interact with our initiative.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect personal data in connection with our website and activities.

We aim to handle personal data fairly, lawfully, transparently and securely, in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), the Nigeria Data Protection Commission's applicable General Application and Implementation Directive 2025 (GAID), and other applicable Nigerian laws.

This policy should be read together with the specific privacy notices displayed on our Contact and Volunteer Application forms.

Echoes of Campus is presented here as a student initiative. This policy does not represent that EOC is an incorporated company, registered association or other formally incorporated legal entity.

3. Personal data we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following categories of information:

A. Contact and communication information

  • Name or preferred name.
  • Email address.
  • Telephone number, where voluntarily provided.
  • The content of messages, enquiries, suggestions, concerns or stories you submit.
  • Your selected reason for contacting us.

B. Volunteer application information

  • Identity and contact details requested by the application form.
  • Information about your interests, skills, experience and availability.
  • Department or role preferences.
  • Answers to application questions.
  • Photographs or other files you voluntarily upload, where requested by the form.

C. Participation and organisational information

Where relevant to your involvement with EOC, we may process:

  • Volunteer or member identification references.
  • Department and assigned responsibilities.
  • Participation, attendance or project-related records.
  • Communications concerning organisational activities.
  • Contributions to interviews, campus stories, media productions or projects.

D. Technical information

When you visit the website, technical information may be processed by the website's hosting or infrastructure providers. Depending on the services and configuration in use, this may include:

  • IP address.
  • Browser and device information.
  • Pages requested.
  • Date and time of requests.
  • Security and diagnostic logs.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal data through ordinary website forms. Please do not submit health information, financial account information, passwords, government identification numbers or other sensitive information unless we have specifically requested it through an appropriate and secure process.

4. How we collect personal data

We may collect personal data:

  1. Directly from you when you complete a website form.
  2. When you contact us by email or another communication channel.
  3. Through volunteer or team registration forms.
  4. When you voluntarily participate in interviews, events, media activities or organisational projects.
  5. Through technical processes operated by our website hosting and service providers.
  6. From information you voluntarily provide to an authorised EOC representative.

We aim to collect only information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

5. Why we use personal data

We may process personal data for the following purposes:

  • Responding to enquiries, questions, suggestions and concerns.
  • Reviewing and managing volunteer applications.
  • Communicating with volunteers and team members.
  • Coordinating departments, interviews, projects and organisational activities.
  • Contacting individuals who request interviews or follow-up.
  • Managing participation in EOC activities.
  • Reviewing and improving our website, forms and services.
  • Protecting the website, users and organisational systems against misuse, fraud and security incidents.
  • Maintaining records where reasonably necessary for accountability, dispute resolution or legal obligations.
  • Publishing stories, photographs, interviews or other contributions only where an appropriate permission or other lawful basis exists.

We will not knowingly use personal data for a purpose incompatible with the purpose for which it was collected without an appropriate lawful basis and any required notice.

6. Lawful basis for processing

Depending on the specific activity, we may rely on one or more lawful bases recognised by the NDPA, including:

  • Consent: where you have freely and specifically agreed to a particular use of your personal data.
  • Legitimate interests: where processing is necessary for a legitimate interest and that interest is not overridden by your rights and freedoms.
  • Contract or pre-contractual steps: where processing is necessary to take steps you request or to perform an agreement with you.
  • Legal obligation: where processing is necessary to comply with a legal requirement.
  • Vital interests or public interest: where the relevant statutory conditions apply.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Where processing is optional, we will not treat silence or inactivity as consent.

7. Contact form and anonymous submissions

Our Contact page allows visitors to contact EOC for different reasons, including suggesting a topic, sharing a story, raising a concern, requesting an interview, asking a question or making a general enquiry.

Where the form provides an anonymous option, the distinction is important:

Anonymous to the public: You may provide identifying or contact information for EOC's authorised administrators to review or use for a response, but that information should not be published publicly merely because you submitted a story or concern.

Completely anonymous: Where this option is provided and correctly configured, the form should not request or transmit your name, email address or telephone number as part of the submission. This means EOC may be unable to identify you or send you a personal response.

An anonymous submission does not guarantee that no technical information is processed by the form provider, hosting provider or other infrastructure services. For example, service providers may process technical logs or security metadata.

Please read the Contact Form Privacy Notice before submitting information.

We may use other service providers for website hosting, email, security, storage or organisational operations. Where a provider processes personal data on our behalf, we aim to use appropriate safeguards and contractual arrangements.

9. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal data.

We may share personal data only where reasonably necessary and lawful, including:

  • With authorised EOC administrators who need the information to perform their responsibilities.
  • With relevant team coordinators where limited information is needed to manage an application or activity.
  • With service providers such as Formspree, Google and website infrastructure providers, where they process information in connection with the services we use.
  • Where disclosure is required by law, a court order or a lawful request from a competent authority.
  • Where necessary to protect individuals, the website, EOC or the rights of others.

Access to raw contact submissions and volunteer application records should be restricted to the authorised administrative team. General members and department leads should not receive unrestricted access to source submissions or registration spreadsheets.

We do not authorise volunteers or team members to disclose private submissions, applicant details or internal records outside their assigned responsibilities.

10. International processing and transfers

Some external service providers may process or store personal data in countries outside Nigeria.

Where personal data is transferred outside Nigeria, the Data Controller must ensure that the transfer is supported by an appropriate legal basis and safeguards under the NDPA, including applicable requirements concerning adequate protection or other permitted transfer grounds.

The use of an international service provider does not remove our responsibility to assess the applicable transfer requirements.

11. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

Retention depends on the type of information and the reason for processing. For example:

  • Contact submissions should be reviewed and deleted or anonymised when no longer needed for follow-up, organisational accountability or a legitimate record-keeping purpose.
  • Volunteer application records should be retained only for the application, onboarding and participation purposes for which they are needed.
  • Records relating to active volunteers may be retained while the individual remains involved and for a limited period afterward where justified.
  • Technical and security logs may be retained for a period determined by the relevant service provider and security requirements.
  • Records required for legal claims or legal obligations may be retained for the applicable period.

Where no legal or other justified time-bound retention requirement applies, we will apply the storage limitation requirements of the NDPA and GAID. Personal data should not be kept indefinitely merely because storage is available.

We will periodically review records and securely delete or anonymise information that is no longer necessary.

12. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, destruction or misuse.

These measures may include:

  • Restricting access to authorised administrators.
  • Using account authentication and access controls.
  • Keeping passwords and access credentials confidential.
  • Reviewing access permissions when roles change.
  • Using reputable third-party service providers.
  • Limiting the collection and circulation of personal information.
  • Taking reasonable steps to secure devices and accounts used for organisational work.
  • Reviewing suspected security incidents and taking appropriate action.

No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We cannot promise absolute security, but we will take appropriate measures in light of the nature and risks of the information processed.

13. Your data protection rights

Subject to the NDPA and any applicable limitations, you may have the right to:

  • Be informed about the processing of your personal data.
  • Request access to personal data concerning you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal data where the legal conditions are satisfied.
  • Request restriction of processing in applicable circumstances.
  • Object to processing where the law gives you that right.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request data portability where the statutory conditions apply.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

To exercise a right, contact us at echoesofcampus01@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity or clarify the request before acting. We will not request unnecessary identity documents.

Some rights are subject to legal exceptions. For example, we may need to retain certain information to comply with law or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

14. Children and young people

EOC is a campus-focused initiative and may receive information from students and other young people.

We do not knowingly seek unnecessary personal data from children. Where a processing activity involves a child or a person who lacks legal capacity to consent, we will apply the requirements of the NDPA concerning children, parental or guardian authorisation where required, and appropriate safeguards.

Do not submit another person's personal information unless you have a lawful reason and any required authority to do so.

Submitting a story, photograph, interview request or other contribution does not automatically grant EOC permission to publish it.

Where we intend to publish identifiable personal information, photographs, recordings, interviews or personal stories, we will seek an appropriate permission or rely on another lawful basis where applicable.

You should avoid including private information about other people in a submission unless you have a lawful basis to share it.

We do not control the privacy practices, security or content of external websites. When you follow an external link, you should review the relevant provider's privacy policy and terms.
