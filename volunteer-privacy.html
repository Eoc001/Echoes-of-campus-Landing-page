1. Why this notice is provided

This notice explains how Echoes of Campus (“EOC”) collects and uses personal information submitted through its Volunteer Application form and related volunteer registration activities.

It supplements the main Privacy Policy.

Please read it before submitting an application.

2. Who is responsible for your information?

Data Controller: Sulaiman Mariam Opeyemi
Initiative: Echoes of Campus
Privacy contact: echoesofcampus01@gmail.com
Website: https://echoesofcampus.vercel.app

3. What information may we collect?

Depending on the questions and fields presented in the application or registration process, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact information.
  • Information about your interests and skills.
  • Your experience and areas of contribution.
  • Your preferred department or role.
  • Your availability.
  • Answers to application questions.
  • A photograph or other file you voluntarily upload, where requested.
  • Information needed to assess, communicate about or manage your participation.

Please provide accurate information and avoid submitting sensitive personal information that is not requested or necessary.

4. Why do we collect this information?

We use volunteer application information to:

  • Receive and review applications.
  • Assess applicants for available volunteer opportunities.
  • Communicate with applicants about their applications.
  • Identify suitable departments, roles and responsibilities.
  • Coordinate onboarding and team participation.
  • Maintain appropriate volunteer and organisational records.
  • Communicate relevant organisational information.
  • Protect the confidentiality, safety and integrity of EOC's operations.

Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance or assignment to a particular role.

5. How do we collect the information?

EOC has used an original volunteer/team member registration process through Google Forms.

That process has involved:

  • A Google Form for registration questions.
  • A connected Google Sheets response record.
  • A Google Drive folder for uploaded photographs and other relevant files.

The website's current Volunteer Application form is separately configured to use Formspree, with the endpoint associated with identifier xyewerva.

The website's Formspree Volunteer Application form is not currently connected to Google Sheets or Google Apps Script.

These are distinct processes. The use of Google Forms and Google Drive for the original registration process should not be understood as evidence that the website's Formspree form is already integrated with Google.

6. Who can access volunteer information?

Raw volunteer application and registration records should be accessible only to the authorised EOC administrative team with a genuine operational need.

Department leads may receive limited information necessary to coordinate an approved applicant or active volunteer. They should not have unrestricted access to raw applications, registration spreadsheets, uploaded photographs or private records.

General members should not have access to confidential applicant records.

Where a photograph or other uploaded file is stored in Google Drive, access should be restricted to authorised administrators. Files should not be made publicly accessible merely for convenience.

7. Photographs and identification

Where you provide a photograph as part of a volunteer registration process, it may be used for internal identification, coordination or other purposes explained to you at the point of collection.

A photograph submitted for internal registration is not automatically authorised for public publication, promotional material or social media.

Where EOC intends to publish an identifiable photograph or use it for a purpose beyond the original registration purpose, an appropriate permission or other lawful basis will be required.

EOC may use an internal volunteer reference or identification code to organise records. Such a reference does not remove the need to protect the underlying personal information.

8. Lawful basis

Depending on the processing activity, EOC may rely on:

  • Your consent.
  • Steps taken at your request before a volunteer arrangement is established.
  • Legitimate interests in managing and coordinating the initiative, where those interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms.
  • A legal obligation where applicable.

Where consent is used as the lawful basis, you may withdraw it. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

9. Third-party service providers

The website Volunteer Application form currently uses Formspree to receive submissions.

The original volunteer/team registration process has used Google Forms, Google Sheets and Google Drive.

These providers may process or store information on systems located outside Nigeria. Their own privacy terms and service arrangements may apply.

EOC must assess the relevant provider arrangements and any international transfer requirements under Nigerian data protection law.

10. Retention

Volunteer information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for application review, onboarding, participation, organisational accountability or a justified legal purpose.

Where an application is unsuccessful or a person does not proceed with participation, the information should be deleted or anonymised when no longer required, subject to any applicable legal claim or other justified retention need.

Information relating to active volunteers may be retained while participation continues and for a limited period afterward where justified.

Photographs and uploaded files should be reviewed periodically and deleted when they are no longer necessary for the stated purpose.

Where no other legal or justified retention period applies, EOC will apply the storage limitation requirements of the NDPA and GAID.

11. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

  • Access personal data held about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion where the legal conditions are satisfied.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Exercise other rights available under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

To make a request, contact echoesofcampus01@gmail.com.

We may need to verify your identity or clarify the request before acting. We will not request unnecessary identity documents.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission at https://ndpc.gov.ng/.
